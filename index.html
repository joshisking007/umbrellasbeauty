A shade for every season you move through

Umbrella's Beauty was built on a simple idea: your beauty routine should move with you, not hold you back. Dry-season glow, rainy-day glam, harmattan lip care — every formula in the line is chosen to work with the way life in Abuja actually feels, month to month.

We test everything on real skin tones before it reaches the catalog, and we keep the range small on purpose — fewer products, chosen properly.
