Our story

Beauty that moves with the year, not against it

Umbrella's Beauty started in Abuja with a simple observation: the products in most kits didn't account for how much a routine actually has to flex — dry-season lips, humid-season shine, harmattan wear-and-tear. We built a small, tightly-edited catalog around that reality instead of around trends.

The name is a small act of protection: an umbrella for whatever the season throws at you, so the beauty underneath stays constant.

What guides what we stock

We keep the range small on purpose. Every addition has to earn its place.

Tested on real skin

Every shade and formula is worn and reviewed before it's listed, across a range of skin tones — not just the ones in the packaging photos.

Built for the climate

Long-wear, waterproof, and hydrating formulas are prioritised, because a Jabi afternoon is a real product test.

Easy to reach us

No account, no checkout maze. Browse, add to bag, and finish the order with a real person on WhatsApp.

Beauty of the seasons

Four seasons, one wardrobe of colour. Our seasonal edits rotate the catalog toward what a look actually needs at that time of year — deeper stains for harmattan wear, glossier finishes for the rainy months — without asking you to relearn your routine every time.
